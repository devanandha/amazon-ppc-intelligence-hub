## Three-minute independent demonstration

1. Enable the built-in fictional-data demo. No private advertising data is necessary.
2. Open **Overview** for conventional metrics and transparent rule recommendations.
3. Open **AI Review** for experimental Isolation Forest outliers and model-dependent conversion uncertainty.
4. Open **Decision Studio**. Change evidence thresholds and observe how the human-review queue responds; these are review safeguards, not automated bid changes.
5. Select a search term and inspect the original rule, uncertainty, unusual-pattern flag and the evidence reason.
6. Review the ACOS sensitivity map. It shows changes in rule labels, **not** a sales forecast.
7. Record an independent judgement in **Practitioner review workspace** and export the feedback CSV. Entries are stored in the current app session only.
8. Download the branded offline HTML evidence report using fictional demo data.

## Independent reviewer questions

- Are the model's unusual patterns useful, or just statistical noise?
- Where is data insufficient to support the original rule action?
- What is missing: attribution, margins, placement or seasonality?
- Would you use the evidence-review workflow? What measurable workload reduction would you expect and how could we test it?
- Are the methods and limitations communicated transparently?

## Claims we do NOT make

No real-world profit uplift; no validated predictive AI or accurate action classifier; no automatic bid changes; no employer/client data in the public demo; no independent recognition or industry endorsement established simply by adding code.

**Evidence plan:** frozen versions, reproducible tests, independently assessed cases, consented pilots, measured adoption and independently published technical evaluation. Critical negative feedback counts as valuable evidence of review, but not positive recognition by itself.
